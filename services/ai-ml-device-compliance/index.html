In short

What does AI/ML medical device compliance involve?

It involves planning how your AI model will change before you submit, managing AI risk inside your ISO 14971 process, and governing AI across your organization. For planned model changes, the plan is a predetermined change control plan (PCCP).

MedDev Solutions drafts the PCCP, the AI risk analysis, and the premarket documentation with your data science and regulatory teams, and implements ISO/IEC 42001, designed to support compliance with that standard.

Sound familiar?

Every delay, handoff, and miscommunication pushes your FDA timeline further out.

AI adds handoffs. Here is one planned model change, without a plan agreed in advance and with one.

What we deliver

What do we deliver, for the device and the organization?

FDA reviews the AI functions in this device. ISO/IEC 42001 looks at how your organization governs AI across every product. We cover both levels.

For your AI-enabled device

The plan, risk analysis, and documentation for the AI functions in your device software.

  • A PCCP drafted with your data science and regulatory teams
  • AI risk management for Software as a Medical Device (SaMD) inside your ISO 14971 process
  • AI-specific risks, such as automation bias, out-of-distribution inputs, and data poisoning
  • A record for third-party foundation models and large language models (LLMs): provenance, version history, update cadence, and output variability
  • A PCCP section for your premarket submission, structured to FDA's PCCP guidance

For your organization

The process that governs AI across your products, from planning to retirement.

  • ISO/IEC 42001:2023 implementation, designed to support compliance with the standard for AI management systems
  • Your development process mapped to FDA's AI/ML guidance
  • Post-market plans that separate operations monitoring (logging, alerting, drift detection) from real-world performance evaluation
  • EU AI Act readiness, for teams also selling into the EU

How an engagement runs

How does an engagement run?

In five phases, scoped to your AI functions. Timing depends on your model, your pathway, and where you start. We scope the work with you on the first call.

  1. 01

    Assess

    We map the AI functions in your software against FDA's AI/ML guidance and ISO/IEC 42001, and list the gaps in the order your submission needs them closed.

  2. 02

    Plan

    AI risk analysis and a draft PCCP, built with your data science and regulatory teams.

  3. 03

    Build

    Validation methods, documentation, and your AI management system, set up with your team.

  4. 04

    Prepare

    Premarket documentation for your AI functions, written with your regulatory team.

  5. 05

    Maintain

    Model changes that fall within your PCCP run under it. Changes outside it go to your regulatory team for a submission decision. Monitoring and real-world performance evaluation feed back into your risk file.
