Service areas

Four areas of work, built for software.

SaMD, software in a device, and embedded firmware. Each area runs on its own or as part of the full program.

FDA Cybersecurity

Threat modeling, cybersecurity risk analysis, Software Bill of Materials (SBOM), and premarket cybersecurity documentation, including your security testing evidence, as the U.S. Food and Drug Administration's (FDA) February 3, 2026 cybersecurity guidance describes. Plus ISO/IEC 27001, SOC 2, HITRUST, HIPAA, and GDPR readiness. Designed to support compliance with IEC 81001-5-1.

Trace chain
  1. Threat
  2. Risk
  3. Test evidence
  4. SBOM
See cybersecurity services→

AI/ML Device Compliance

FDA guidance for AI-enabled device software, ISO/IEC 42001 implementation, and Predetermined Change Control Plans (PCCPs). A PCCP is a plan agreed with FDA before market that defines which changes you may make after market and how they are validated, so planned updates don't need a new submission each time.

Planned model change
  1. Model v1
  2. PCCP
  3. Model v2

Software Development & IEC 62304 SQA

Medical device software development for SaMD and digital health. IEC 62304 lifecycle execution and documentation, senior software quality assurance (SQA), verification and validation, and a Secure Software Development Lifecycle (SSDLC). Firmware counts: it's embedded software.
