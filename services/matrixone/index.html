In short

What does MedDev Solutions do for MatrixOne customers?

MedDev Solutions resells and implements MatrixOne. You buy the license through us, and the same team configures MatrixOne to your processes and writes the validation protocols with your team.

Validation is planned for your intended use and scaled to risk, as FDA's "Computer Software Assurance for Production and Quality Management System Software" guidance (February 3, 2026) recommends.

Sound familiar?

Can't your internal team handle this?

It can, alongside its day job. Someone still has to configure MatrixOne, document your intended use, and produce validation evidence. MatrixOne's published guidance asks customers to document their own intended use and the related risks. The U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) "Computer Software Assurance for Production and Quality Management System Software" guidance (February 3, 2026) treats vendor validation work as a starting point.

How an implementation runs

How does an implementation run?

In four phases, from process mapping to hand-over. Timing depends on your processes, your existing records, and which parts of MatrixOne you use. We agree the scope with your RA/QA lead before configuration starts.

  1. 01

    Scope

    We map your processes to MatrixOne and agree what to configure first.

  2. 02

    Configure

    MatrixOne set up to your processes, with your team reviewing each step.

  3. 03

    Validate

    Intended use and risks documented, IQ/OQ/PQ protocols written with your team.

  4. 04

    Hand over

    Your validation protocols and records are written and ready for your QA team to review and approve. Your team runs the system.
