Regulatory strategy and submissions

Your regulatory strategy and submission, written with your team.

We help you work out your software's U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) classification, plan the pathway, and write the submission documentation with you: 510(k), De Novo, and EU Medical Device Regulation (MDR).

In short

What does regulatory strategy for a software device include?

It includes working out your software's FDA classification, choosing the pathway (510(k), De Novo, or PMA), and writing the submission documentation and Design History File (DHF) behind it. FDA makes every classification and marketing authorization decision.

MedDev Solutions writes that documentation with your team, assembles the eSTAR package, and supports Pre-Submission (Q-Sub) meetings and responses to FDA additional information requests.

Sound familiar?

How is a consultant different if your team is still the one doing the work?

The file still has to be written. The Design History File (DHF) still needs the current version of every document your submission points to. We write the submission documentation with your team and keep the DHF in step with your software.

What we deliver

What do we deliver: the submission, or the quality system behind it?

Your product needs a pathway and a file. Your organization needs a quality system that keeps both current. We handle the two together, for software devices.

For your submission

The pathway, the classification, and the documentation for your software device.

  • FDA classification of your software (Software as a Medical Device (SaMD) or software in a device)
  • Pathway strategy: 510(k) or De Novo
  • FDA submission documentation and your Design History File (DHF)
  • FDA, EU MDR and CE marking support

For your organization

The quality and regulatory system behind every submission you make.

  • ISO 13485 quality management system implementation
  • ISO 14971 risk management
  • Fractional Regulatory Affairs and Quality teams
  • Electronic quality management system (eQMS) and application lifecycle management (ALM) digitization
  • Regulatory due-diligence support for investors

How an engagement runs

How does an engagement run?

In five phases, from classification to post-market. Timing depends on your software, your pathway, and where you start. We scope the work with you on the first call.

  1. 01

    Classify

    We help you work out the FDA classification of your software and document the rationale.

  2. 02

    Plan

    Pathway strategy (510(k) or De Novo, plus EU MDR if you sell in the EU) and a submission plan each team can work to.

  3. 03

    Build the file

    Submission documentation and DHF, written with your team as the software and records come in.

  4. 04

    Submit and respond

    Submission support, and help preparing responses when FDA asks questions.

  5. 05

    Maintain

    Your quality system, DHF, and post-market surveillance kept current after FDA marketing authorization.
