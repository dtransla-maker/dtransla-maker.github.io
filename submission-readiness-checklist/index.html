Free checklist for SaMD and software medical device teams

Five handoffs to own before your FDA submission.

Development, quality, regulatory, testing, and cybersecurity each own a piece of your submission to the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA). This checklist covers the handoffs between them.

See the five handoffs

What's inside

Five handoffs.What to check at each.

For each handoff between two of your teams, here is the question to ask. Run it with your teams before your file goes in.

  1. Handoff 1

    Development → Quality

    Do your requirements, code, and IEC 62304 records describe the same software, at the same version?

  2. Handoff 2

    Quality → Regulatory

    Does your Design History File (DHF) hold every document your submission points to, in its current version?

  3. Handoff 3

    Regulatory → Testing

    Does your verification and validation evidence support each claim your submission makes about the software?

  4. Handoff 4

    Testing → Cybersecurity

    Do your security tests trace back to your threat model and cybersecurity risk assessment? FDA recommends traceability between those, your Software Bill of Materials (SBOM), and your testing documentation (FDA premarket cybersecurity guidance, February 3, 2026).

  5. Handoff 5

    Cybersecurity → Development

    Does your threat model change when your architecture changes? FDA recommends threat modeling throughout the design process (same guidance).

You can answer 0 of 5 today.

Who it's for

You're a Founder, VP R&D, or VP RA-QA at a US company building software medical devices. You have an FDA submission on your plan.

  • Your development, quality, regulatory, testing, and cybersecurity work sits with different teams or vendors
  • You're the one connecting them today
  • Your product is software: Software as a Medical Device (SaMD), software in a device, or embedded firmware
